### Evidence tags
**Audit Verified** (`tag-green-sm`: bg #dcfce7, color #15803d, border rgba(21,128,61,.25)) = directly confirmed by browser/page-source/schema audit. **Inferred** (`tag-amber-sm`: bg #fff3cd, color #92400e) = logical conclusion, not tested. ### REVIEW-MARKUP RULE
**Never list missing aggregateRating/review markup as a gap and never recommend adding it for a business's own reviews** (deliberate omission is correct for LocalBusiness/Organization sites — director of GBP's standard). Banned everywhere (tiles, stat cards, paragraphs, definitions, scorecard rows, hero facts, AI Visibility, Findings, Opportunities, Solution, Why Act Now): any "review markup / review-rating markup / aggregateRating absent" finding; "machine-readable"; "reviews excluded from rich results"; "invisible to Google"; "highest-impact schema fix". Never claim review markup yields stars, rich results, CTR or AI benefit. If a client's schema already contains aggregateRating/review for its own reviews → flag as a RISK to the director, never a selling point. Do not count it toward AI_VIS_GAPS.
Verified vs undocumented (checked against Google's official docs Oct 18 2026): own-business/widget reviews are "self-serving", ineligible for star results — **supported**; "gains nothing visible" — partly; markup mismatch → manual action — partly (documented consequence = page loses rich-result eligibility, not necessarily the whole site); local-pack stars come from the Business Profile — partly/not explicit; hasMap + sameAs "link the entity to its GBP" — **not documented by Google** (state effects as Inferred only; never say Google's Knowledge Graph "uses sameAs" or thin sameAs "reduces AI confidence" as fact).

### OVERALL SCORE METHOD
- **Overall = equal-weight average of the scorecard category rows, 1 decimal.** Compute in code; update EVERY occurrence (DHAP summary `score-num`, scorecard OVERALL row + bar width = score×10 %, hot sheet `score-big`, teaser "Score logged: X/10", slider `.score-fill` width = score×10 %).
- **Weakest-link status rule:** the overall status label comes from the LOWEST of the eight category scores, never from the average. Lowest 5.0 or below = **Critical**. Lowest above 5.0 and below 7.0 = **Major Issues**. Lowest 7.0 to 8.9 = **Needs Improvement**. Lowest 9.0 or higher = **Fully Optimized**. Use exactly these four labels in every place the overall status appears. The overall score shown is still the equal-weight average of all eight categories. Compute the status in code from the lowest row and update EVERY occurrence.
- State the method on the pages — DHAP scorecard footnote: "Category ratings are scored from direct audit findings against a fixed rubric. Photo and post cadence are rated against a standard we set — one new photo per week per profile; 8 posts a month for Sales and 4 each for Service and Parts — not a Google requirement. OVERALL status cell shows the icon and label from the lowest category: 🔴 Critical · 🟠 Major Issues · 🟡 Needs Improvement · 🟢 Fully Optimized, followed by the small gray note "(status set by the lowest category: [lowest row] is [n]/10)" (name the actual low row). Hot sheet caption under slider (`.score-method`, .74rem italic 600 #334155): "Overall = average of 8 audited categories · status set by the lowest category ([lowest row]: [n]/10)".
- Re-rate/add/remove a row → recompute and re-check the weakest-link rule.

### CATEGORY RUBRICS — v13 (locked Oct 19 2026, requested by Sean)
Each category is out of 10, built only from evidence the audit can observe, recorded with method + date. **A missing department is penalized in Profile Completeness only**; Claimed & Verified, Photos, Reviews and Posts judge the listings that exist, **weighted Sales 50 % / Service 25 % / Parts 25 %, renormalized over the listings that exist** (Sales only = 100 % Sales). Schema rows judge the website's schema; listing rows judge Google listings (both views). Never score review markup. Schema effects stay Inferred — the schema rows score presence only. Photo ages and review ages are Google's approximate labels as of the audit date.
**1. GBP Claimed & Verified** — 8: every existing listing is claimed and verified (averaged) · 2: no duplicate or unclaimed listings for the dealership or its departments turn up in the audit search. ≤2 = the main Sales listing is unclaimed or unverified.
**2. Profile Completeness** — Structure 4 points: Sales listing exists 2 · Service listing exists 1 · Parts listing exists 1 (missing = 0). Information 6 points: each existing listing earns up to 2 × its checklist percentage (Sales 2, Service 2, Parts 2; missing = 0). A **combined "Service & Parts" listing counts as BOTH Service and Parts** (structure 1 + 1, information 2 + 2; its checklist percentage applies to both) and can therefore reach 10 with a complete Sales listing. The row is 0 only when none of the three listings exist; Service or Parts without a Sales listing earn their own points and Sales earns 0. Maximums at 100 % complete: Sales only 4 · Sales + Service (or Parts) 7 · Sales + combined 10 · all three 10. **Checklist (8 items; the 8th applies only to the Service or combined listing, so Sales and Parts are out of 7, Service out of 8):** primary and relevant additional categories · hours for that department · phone for that department · website link · services or products listed · business description · attributes · scheduling link (Service listing). **Connection finding (finding only, no points):** on a properly built main Sales listing a bold "Departments:" line shows a separate link for each department listing (e.g. "[Dealership] Parts · [Dealership] Service Department"); a combined listing shows as one link in the same place. Raise the finding when a department listing exists but is not shown on that line, or the line is absent.
**3. Photos & Visual Presence** — 6 cadence: per existing listing, min(new photos in the last 30 days ÷ 4, 1) × 6 (benchmark: one new photo per week per profile), weighted 50/25/25 · 2: logo and cover photo (1 point each; record which was seen in the audit — one not seen earns 0 for that half) · 2: coverage (exterior; interior or team; department-relevant). Photos attached to posts count as photos.
**4. Reviews & Reputation** — 3 Google rating (4.5+ = 3; 4.0–4.4 = 2; 3.5–3.9 = 1; below 3.5 = 0) · 3 recent flow: a review inside the window per listing — Sales 15 days (1.5 pts), Service 30 days (0.75), Parts 90 days (0.75), renormalized over existing listings; full credit inside the window, half inside twice the window, 0 beyond · 2 owner responses, scored pro-rata on the 10 newest reviews (2 × replies ÷ 10) · 1 Facebook rating within 0.5 of Google — only when Facebook shows a star rating; when it shows a recommendation percentage instead, this point is dropped and the row is rescaled to 10 (points earned ÷ 9 × 10) · 1 real reviews visibly shown on the dealership's own website (rendered-site check; never marked up).
**5. GBP Posts & Engagement** — 6 cadence: per existing listing, min(posts in the last 30 days ÷ benchmark, 1) × 6 with benchmark 8 Sales / 4 Service / 4 Parts, weighted 50/25/25 · 2 recency (last post within 7 days = 2; within 14 days = 1; older = 0) · 2 variety (offers, events, calls to action). Posts with a photo count as a post and as a photo.
**6. Google Maps / NAP Consistency** — 4: name, address and phone on the Google listing match the website · 4: a sample of five directories (Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp, Facebook, the Mitsubishi dealer locator) agree — a directory agrees when its name, street address and phone match the website; a different phone number on a directory (for example the locator's central number) counts as a mismatch and gets a finding; a city name that is the common postal name for the same address (for example Rochester for Greece, NY 14626 on Apple Maps) counts as a match · 1: every existing department listing has its own correct phone (not applicable = earned) · 1: map pin and directions link are correct.
**7. LocalBusiness Schema (structure and coverage)** — 3 page coverage: JSON-LD present on home, About, Service, Parts, Directions (pro-rata) · 1 appropriate business type · 3 department entities (Sales, Service, Parts, 1 each) · 1 markup parses cleanly · 2 each department entity carries its own page address (1) and its own phone (1).
**8. Schema Field Completeness** — 3 core fields on the main entity (name, address, telephone, url, hours, location coordinates; pro-rata) · 1 logo or image · 2 sameAs (1 includes the Google Business Profile / Google Maps link, 1 other official profiles) · 2 areaServed · 1 hasMap or legacy map on the main entity · 1 services or offers.
**Evidence capture (mandatory before scoring):** per listing — claimed/verified status, checklist items, photos in the last 30 days, posts in the last 30 days and last-post date, latest review date, owner responses on the 10 newest reviews, logo and cover photo seen or not, whether the "Departments:" line shows; plus the five-directory sample, Facebook rating, rendered-site review check and the schema checks above. Anything not recorded = audit incomplete: do not publish a score. Do not publish a new overall for a dealership until every row is scored from recorded evidence.

### RULE K — EVIDENCE POPUPS (standing rule, v14.4; DHAP and Digital Health Alert)
**Every claim the report shows in a tile, card, stat card or hero fact has an evidence popup — all of them, never some of a set.** A group where three cards show proof and the fourth does not looks suspicious; transparency is the rule. The popup opens inside the report (the reader never leaves it) and shows the proof the audit captured for that exact claim.
1. **Scope.** DHAP: hero facts and the callout bullets (Summary, AI Visibility), AI Visibility cards, every Findings card (gaps, opportunities, what's working) and the dealer-specific Why Act Now cards. Alert: every KPI tile (screen 2) and every stat card (screen 3). **Exempt (no audit claim):** the omni-social pitch cards, the methodology card, the CLIKsearch closing box, and the blurred locked findings 06–08 (they stay locked). Table rows (scorecard, AI signal table, Opportunities table) are not covered yet; a later version extends the rule to them. A new report that adds a tile or card adds its popup in the same build.
2. **Content.** Per claim: one or more cropped screenshots from this audit's own Chrome captures, each with a caption saying what to look at, or — for website schema — an excerpt of the parsed JSON-LD; plus a note with the date and method. Aggregates (for example AI_VIS_GAPS) list their components. Capture the evidence during the audit and keep it with the evidence record; a claim whose proof cannot be captured is not published (same principle as the incomplete-evidence rule).
3. **Privacy.** Crop to the claim's area. Never show customer or reviewer names (blur them), the auditor's account name, avatar, notifications or any other signed-in detail, or personal data of private individuals.
4. **Behaviour.** Hover previews (non-blocking, never covers the neighbouring card); click or tap pins and enlarges it with a close button, Esc closes; on phones it opens as a bottom sheet; cards are keyboard-focusable. A hint line sits above each group: "Hover over a highlighted number to preview the audit screenshot behind it; click or tap to enlarge." Images are embedded as compressed data URIs (JPEG, about 480 px wide) so the file stays self-contained.
5. **Implementation.** `scripts/proof_popup.py` builds the CSS + script block from a small evidence spec (keys, notes, images, code excerpts) and a list of text rules that map each card's title or label to a key; insert it before `</body>`. `scripts/check_proof_popups.py` opens the built page in a browser, lists every tile and card with its key, and fails on any non-exempt card without one. Run it before delivery.
7. **Ask AI runs (no browser screenshots of its own) — v14.7.** Ask AI builds the same popups with the **proof service** (`shot/`; address in the GHL Custom Value **"DHAP Proof URL"**, team code from **"DHAP Team Code"** in the `X-DHAP-Code` header). Per claim: (a) **picture proof first** — `GET {DHAP Proof URL}/screenshot?url=…&preset=…` (presets `gmaps_panel` for the Google Maps listing, `apple_panel` for Apple Maps, `full` for others); use the returned `image` and its `captured_at` time; (b) when the reply is `"status": "blocked"` or `"error"`, or the page can't be pictured signed-out, use **data proof**: the exact values from the source with the time read and where they came from — Google listing facts from the key service `/lookup` ("Google's official listing data, read 2026-10-21 14:14 UTC"), website schema from `GET {DHAP Proof URL}/schema?url=…` (quote the JSON-LD lines), and anything else as the browser agent's word-for-word reading with the page address.
